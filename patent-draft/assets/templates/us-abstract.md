# U.S. abstract — working template

## Text

[One paragraph, no more than 150 words: identify the technical subject, central
organization or steps, and technical operation. Avoid claim phraseology, alleged
merits, speculative uses, and comparisons with prior art.]

## Control

- [ ] 150 words or fewer.
- [ ] One paragraph directed to the technical disclosure.
- [ ] Consistent with the title, claims, description, and drawings.
- [ ] No unsupported novelty, benefit, or comparative assertion.
- [ ] Not used as the sole support for a claim limitation.
