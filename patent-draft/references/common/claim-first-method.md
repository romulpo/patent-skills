# Claim-first drafting method

Draft a claim plan before a full specification.

For each planned independent claim, record:

- claim category and intended subject;
- commercial or operational value it should preserve;
- minimum technically coherent feature combination;
- actor and system boundary;
- technical result and feature-to-result causal chain;
- supplied support for every limitation;
- known substitutions and variants that are actually supported;
- dependent fallback positions;
- deliberate gaps and unresolved breadth questions.

Then draft the claim and a limitation table. Do not hide scope choices in prose.
Explain when a narrower feature is used for support, technical coherence, or a
profile constraint. Do not broaden by replacing disclosed structure with a desired
result unless the supplied material supports the broader teaching.

The description follows the claims, but it must remain an authentic disclosure. It
may not be backfilled with invented examples solely to support desired breadth.
