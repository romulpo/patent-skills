## Scope and limits

- The profile covers substantive drafting for a U.S. nonprovisional utility
  application. It is not a filing checklist and does not calculate fees,
  deadlines, entity status, priority, inventorship, or disclosure duties.
- The detailed `patent-audit` skill remains the repository's filing-oriented U.S.
  review tool. Do not silently import its severity labels into drafting.
- Chemistry, biotechnology, pharmaceuticals, sequence listings, deposits, and
  plant or design practice require specialist source extensions.
- The annual CFR edition is a stable reference snapshot, not a substitute for the
  current eCFR. Complete the verification gate before a filing-facing handoff.
