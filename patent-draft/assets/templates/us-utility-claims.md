# U.S. utility claims — working template

## Claim-family plan

| Independent claim | Statutory category | Subject and boundary | Evidence IDs | Scope reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Independent claim 1

1. A [machine, manufacture, composition, or method], comprising:
   [supported limitations and relationships].

## Dependent claims

2. The [short claim subject] of claim 1, further comprising [supported further limitation].

## Additional independent family — only when supported

[Do not add method, system, or non-transitory-medium families mechanically. Create
a separate limitation and support map for each family.]

## Control

- [ ] Every limitation has exact support in the description and support map.
- [ ] Each claim has one coherent statutory category and a clear actor/system boundary.
- [ ] Dependencies refer backward and further limit without replacing parent matter.
- [ ] Antecedent basis, terminology, units, ranges, and alternatives are consistent.
- [ ] Any possible § 112(f) limitation is intentional and mapped to corresponding disclosure.
- [ ] Software functions map to disclosed algorithms, not result-only black boxes.
- [ ] A medium claim excludes transitory signals and has affirmative disclosure support.
- [ ] No claim text depends on an abstract or drafting note for missing disclosure.
