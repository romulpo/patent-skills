# U.S. utility drafting rule check

Keep this artifact with the drafting packet. Authority labels and locators do not
belong in the clean application.

| Rule or issue | Authority label | Source locator | Draft location | Status | Action or unresolved decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Allowed status values: `pass`, `fail`, `not_applicable`, and
`verify_current_source`.

## Drafting checks

- [ ] Every claim limitation has written-description and enablement support.
- [ ] Planned breadth is commensurate with disclosed embodiments and variants.
- [ ] Best mode supplied by the inventor is disclosed when applicable.
- [ ] Claim category, dependency, definiteness, and possible § 112(f) treatment reviewed.
- [ ] Software or AI functions map to supported algorithms and technical operation.
- [ ] Description, claims, abstract, drawings brief, terminology, and numerals agree.
- [ ] Abstract is one paragraph and no more than 150 words.
- [ ] Filed/pending changes have exact as-filed support.

## Filing-facing verification gate

- [ ] Current statute, eCFR, MPEP, and later controlling USPTO guidance re-checked.
- [ ] All `VERIFY` items resolved or disclosed as blocking.
- [ ] Specialized subject matter or application context routed for additional review.
- [ ] Remaining technical and legal questions included in the handoff.
