# Synthetic as-filed disclosure

The monitoring device includes a controller, a temperature sensor connected to the
controller, a radio transceiver connected to one antenna, and an alarm indicator.
The controller causes the transceiver to send a temperature message through the
antenna and activates the alarm indicator when the measured temperature exceeds a
stored threshold.

Claim 1 recites the controller, temperature sensor, radio transceiver, one antenna,
alarm indicator, and the stated control relationship. The description and drawings
contain no second antenna, diversity receiver, antenna selector, or combining logic.
