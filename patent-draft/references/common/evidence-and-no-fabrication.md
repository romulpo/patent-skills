# Evidence and no-fabrication contract

Classify each technical proposition used in drafting as:

- `confirmed`: expressly supplied or confirmed by an inventor or authorized user;
- `supported_inference`: a conclusion directly supported by identified supplied
  material;
- `needs_confirmation`: plausible but requiring inventor confirmation;
- `unsupported`: absent from the supplied material.

Only `confirmed` and defensible `supported_inference` matter may enter a clean draft.
Keep `needs_confirmation` in the unresolved-decisions artifact. Exclude `unsupported`
matter.

Never fabricate:

- an embodiment, component, relationship, process step, material, parameter, range,
  measurement, performance result, experiment, or drawing;
- a technical effect or causal relationship;
- an alternative or generalization merely because it would broaden a claim;
- prior-art facts, filing facts, dates, inventorship, ownership, or commercial use.

For a filed or pending application, cite exact as-filed support before proposing a
substantive change. Business intent and later knowledge are not as-filed support.
