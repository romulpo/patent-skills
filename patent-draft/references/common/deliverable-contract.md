# Drafting deliverable contract

Return these artifacts for a complete drafting run:

1. clean application draft;
2. claims-only version;
3. invention thesis;
4. claim plan and limitation tables;
5. support map for every claim limitation;
6. terminology map;
7. drawings brief and reference-numeral table;
8. unresolved inventor decisions and missing evidence;
9. change log for a revision or translation/adaptation;
10. concise profile-specific limitations and warnings.

Keep editable working artifacts separate from source evidence. Never overwrite the
user's source material. Label the output as an AI-assisted draft, not a filed
application, legal opinion, patentability conclusion, or assurance of allowance,
validity, enforceability, or commercial coverage.
