# Synthetic inventor disclosure: inspection latch

This fictional disclosure is supplied only for skill evaluation.

The latch includes a housing 10, a slider 12 received in a straight channel of the
housing, and a compression spring 14 between the slider and an end wall of the
housing. A transparent inspection window 16 is fixed over the channel. A red region
18 on the slider is visible through the window when the slider is retracted and is
hidden by the housing when the slider is extended.

The spring biases the slider toward the extended position. Pulling the slider
against the spring exposes the red region through the window, giving a visible
indication that the latch is not engaged. The window was implemented in one
prototype using polycarbonate and in another using glass. No other window material
has been evaluated. Figure 1 shows the housing 10, slider 12, spring 14, window 16,
and red region 18 in the extended position. Figure 2 shows the retracted position.
